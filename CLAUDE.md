# GymOps

Design docs for a gym management system: subscriptions, direct debit billing,
parent/child accounts, coach class management, and room/equipment/coach scheduling
for a client's gym.

## Files

- `entity-relationships.md` — data model (ERD + design rationale, open questions)
- `use-cases.md` — who can do what, by role (English)
- `use-cases.es.md` — Spanish translation of the above
- `ux-design-principles.md` — UX methodology notes (OOUX, Material Design 3)
- `gym-saas-market-landscape.md` — unverified third-party market research on gym SaaS
